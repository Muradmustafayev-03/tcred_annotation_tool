Before you begin

What you will judge

Each practice or research card contains a question, a candidate answer, and sometimes evidence or candidate graph paths. Your task is not to answer the question yourself. Your task is to judge the supplied candidate and each supplied support component.

Plan about 70 minutes. Practice takes about 10 minutes; the 20 research answers take about 60 minutes. Use a desktop or laptop computer. Mobile layout is not guaranteed for this study.

The one decision rule for all fields

Can the displayed material decide this field? Judge only what is shown. Feeling unsure is not itself a label.
Cannot judgeNo. Required information is missing, ambiguous, unreadable, or conflicting.
YesYes, and the field's requirement is fully satisfied.
PartialYes, and a meaningful part is satisfied, but a material part is missing or mixed.
NoYes, and the central requirement fails.

Key distinction: choose Partial when you have enough information to identify exactly what is incomplete. Choose Cannot judge when the displayed information does not let you decide.

Each field asks a different question

Temporal correctIs the timing of each claim the candidate actually makes valid for the requested date or operation? A date attached only to another entity or relation cannot decide this field.
Evidence supports claim contentPretend all dates are hidden. Do the evidence words support the candidate's stated factual claims? If hiding dates leaves mutually exclusive claims that cannot be resolved, use Cannot judge.
Citation time coverageIgnoring citation content, do the displayed intervals of evidence marked cited cover the required time?
Graph evidence sufficientDo the displayed paths, judged together, contain every node, direction, relation, hop, and time needed for the candidate's stated claims?
Answer correctAfter the reference is revealed, does the candidate supply the requested result completely and correctly? A correct setup fact alone is not a partial answer.
Answer/refusal decision appropriateWas the candidate right to answer rather than refuse, or to refuse rather than answer?

A stale sentence can therefore be Yes for evidence content but No for temporal correctness and citation time coverage. These labels are intentionally independent.

  • Missing list item: Stage 1 can still be Yes when every claim the candidate states is supported and in time; mark the incomplete answer Partial after the reference is revealed.
  • Irrelevant dated citation: Temporal can be Cannot judge, Evidence can be No, and Citation time coverage can still be Yes when the citation interval is in range but concerns another claim.
  • Refusal: A justified refusal can be Answer = No but Decision = Yes: it withholds the requested result, yet correctly avoids inventing one from insufficient shown material.

Dates, operators, and source wording

Treat displayed interval boundaries as inclusive. At any time means any overlap; throughout means the whole interval is covered; after and before use strict cutoffs; and by includes the cutoff. Include every tied item for first, last, previous, or next. Tied items may be listed in any order, but words such as then or followed by assert a sequence. That sequence is wrong when both items have the same timestamp.

A dated Wikidata snapshot asks what that snapshot recorded, not what is true today. A dated document revision asks what that revision stated; its revision date does not prove that the statement was world-valid on that date.

Some cards intentionally contain no evidence or no graph path. The corresponding judgment fields are omitted automatically. Do not invent missing material and do not report an error merely because an optional component is absent.

When several graph paths are shown, judge the set collectively. One complete path, or several paths that together provide every required connection, can be sufficient. An unrelated extra path does not make sufficient graph evidence wrong.

1Review question, candidate, evidence, paths, and source-date note
2Judge every visible evidence-stage field independently
3Lock those judgments and reveal the reference
4Judge answer correctness and the answer/refusal decision
Use only this page. Do not search the web, use AI tools, ask another person, or rely on unstated prior knowledge. Outside sources may describe a later or different world state and would invalidate the comparison. This is not a memory test: use Cannot judge when the shown material cannot decide a visible field.

Practice answers are excluded from research results. The study stores only aggregate attempts and failed attempts for the two independently checked practice cards, not practice labels or interaction traces.
